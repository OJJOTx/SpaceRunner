A SCAVENGER’S FIELD GUIDE

Make a little trouble.

◆ Find your fortuneMint crystals = 1 cargo. Gold = 3. Carry 12, then bank at any active bank. Full bags earn +4 bonus points.

◎ Pulse & plunderSpace / PULSE knocks up to 4 cargo from nearby pilots and breaks supply crates. Recharge: 4 seconds.

↗ Get out of thereShift / DASH launches you in your movement direction. Recharge: 3.2 seconds. Use it to dodge red meteor circles.

✧ Take a power tripM = magnet pickup range, S = pulse/meteor shield, B = speed boost. Power-ups last 8.5 seconds.

◇ Risk the vaultThe center vault opens for 12 seconds every 35 seconds and pays double. Everyone wants a piece.

⌂ Follow the banksFour banks appear in random clear locations. They relocate one at a time, with an 8-second warning. Banks wait while a pilot is nearby. Your arrow and minimap always track their current position.

⚙ Fight the scrappersThieves steal 2 cargo; bruisers drop 3 and briefly stun you. Leave their orange attack circles or pulse to interrupt. Thieves take 2 hits, bruisers 3. Defeat them to recover stolen loot plus a bonus. Bank rings are safe from NPC attacks.

☄ Watch the skyRed circles warn of an impact 2.4 seconds ahead. Meteors scatter valuable crystals. Dodge first, loot second.
